## Listening sockets

The management surface is offered over the relay leg only, which dials out. The
model surface is the only thing that listens, on loopback and — when direct
access is on — the WireGuard interface. `private` or `public` on any row means
something is reachable that should not be.

## Command trail

Every admitted command is chained to the one before it, and the head is reported
to the paired Home. Chaining catches an edit; the Home holding an earlier head is
what makes that catch survive the box being the editor.
